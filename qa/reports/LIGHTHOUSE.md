The target and budget apply to the returning visit; first-visit rows are reported, not judged. ## What is left for the workspace (not done yet)

The 85 target is met by no workspace page yet (best 83). Real content appears at about 3.8–4.3 s on the simulated slow
phone; the remaining time is mostly the download before the first page can draw (about 440 KB: the 90 KB stylesheet
bundle and about 250 KB of start-up scripts on a slow 4G link), then the session and start-up data calls. The next
steps, in order of size:
1. Tried on 25 Nov and reverted (D-076): loading the support panel, staff chat, live toasts, phone notifications and
   tour just after the first page saved about 20 KB but scored lower (67–80), because their loading still falls inside
   the measured start-up window and adds a request. The notices panel cannot move later at all: it finds the bell by
   its English label before the page is translated.
2. Draw the first page sooner. Tried on 25 Nov and not kept (D-077): fetching the opened page's code while the session
   is checked and loading branding alongside the start-up data cut Enquiries' largest paint by about 0.2 s in two
   paired runs, but run-to-run noise on this machine (blocking time 490–1,000 ms for the same code) hid any score gain,
   so it was not worth another full verification round. The bigger version was then done (D-078): the first page now
   waits only for its own start-up data and the account status, and the other start-up calls finish in the background.
   In alternating paired runs the page was ready 0.2–0.3 s sooner on Command Centre, Enquiries and Money and scores
   rose by 1–6 points.
3. Shrink the stylesheet bundle (474 KB unpacked): most rules belong to pages that are not open.
