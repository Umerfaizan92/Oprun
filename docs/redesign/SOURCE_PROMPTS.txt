1. Deep Inventory Re-Analysis (Source 6615f96)
The inventory documents 60 business pages (B01–B09) and 34 founder pages. Core strength is the end-to-end service
pipeline (enquiry → quote → booking → job/allocation → completion → invoice → payment) with approval gates, AI
assistance, Australian compliance focus (GST, work rights, offshore acknowledgements) and multi-tenant isolation.
Weakness is fragmentation: too many parallel surfaces for communications, marketing, SEO and finance that increase
cognitive load and training cost.
Critical overlaps requiring remapping:

   • Communications: Inbox (all channels), Unified Inbox, Email Centre, Calls & SMS, Team Chat — different stores and
   scopes but confusing labels.
   • Growth/Campaigns: Growth Engine, Campaign Drafts, Campaign Studio, Media Studio, Content Studio.
   • Website/SEO: 13+ pages (Command Centre, Audit, Keywords, Google, Content Studio, Builder, Local, Analytics,
   Reports, Website & Domain, Search & AI Visibility, Search Connection Request, Backlinks).
   • Money: Profit & Payments, Invoices, Quotes & Invoices, Payments & Refunds, Bank Reconciliation, Reports & GST,
   Finance Settings.
   • People: Workers & Compliance, Staff Accounts & Roles, Hiring, Offshore Staff — conceptually distinct but poorly
   hierarchical.
   • Automation: Today AI Automations, Setup Automation Settings, Founder AI automations.
Strengths to preserve and elevate:

   • Approval-required model for external/consequential actions (messages, publish, payments, schedule, permissions).
   • Eligibility engine for worker allocation with skills/compliance checks.
   • Native AI receptionist and operations tasks with human review.
   • Australian-specific: GST/BAS draft, work rights guidance, privacy acknowledgements.
   • Tenant isolation enforced server-side (session determines business); navigation hiding is not the auth mechanism.
   • Founder Console cleanly separated from business workspace.


2. Live Site Observations (12 Nov 2026)
Public homepage (oprun.ai) positions Oprun as an AI Operating System for service businesses: approval gates,
plain-language AI, on-site use, plans (Starter / Operations / Scale). Design is clean and practical. Workspace URL redirects to
sign-in.html. Sign-in page shows: Email, Password, Remember email, optional Authenticator code, links for forgot
password/email, Operator sign-in, Create account. Messaging emphasises secure workspace, MFA, audit-ready, one
connected workspace. No public workspace data is exposed.
From summariser capture of the authenticated workspace view (zero-state): Command Centre with readiness scores, AI
operations brief, core workflow steps, sections for People, Jobs, Money, Content Studio, Connections, Trust, etc. Heavy use
of forms, AI “Ask” buttons, empty states and Australian catalogue references. Impression: feature-dense, owner-controlled,
compliance-aware, but visually busy and form-heavy for daily use.

Immediate UX issues visible even in zero state:

   • Too many top-level sections and “Ask AI” entry points competing for attention.
• Empty-state messaging is functional but not guided enough toward the next high-value action.
• Sign-in is solid but could surface progressive onboarding more clearly after first login.
• No visible mobile-optimised preview in the captured view; field-worker experience needs priority.
3. Recommended Remapping, Consolidation & Removals
Target: reduce business navigation to 6–7 primary groups with progressive disclosure. Most complexity moves into tabs,
contextual panels or role-filtered views. Founder Console remains fully separate.


Proposed primary navigation (business, role-filtered):
  • Today — Command Centre (attention, AI entry, approvals, automations summary)
  • Work — Unified Pipeline (Enquiries / Quotes / Bookings / Jobs with stage filters + eligibility) + Stock
  • Customers — Directory with full timeline (comms, jobs, photos, invoices)
  • People — Workers & Compliance + Staff Accounts & Roles + Hiring (owner/admin) + Offshore
  • Communications — Unified Inbox (all channels + folders) + Call Handling + AI Receptionist + Meetings + Team Chat
  • Growth — Growth Hub (plan, calendar, campaigns, consent) + Media Studio + SEO Command (tabs) + Website Builder
  • Money — Ledger / Documents / Payments / Reconciliation / Reports & GST / Settings
  • Setup — Brand & Settings + Connections + Import + Export + Trust & Security + Automation + Help + Manual + Plans


Explicit removal / deprecation recommendations:
  • Standalone “Inbox (all channels)” page — remove; route to Unified Inbox.
  • Separate top-level Email Centre — become a folder/filter inside Unified Inbox.
  • Generic “Campaign Drafts” — merge into Campaign Studio / Growth Hub drafts.
  • Older “Search & AI Visibility” assessment workspace — fold into SEO Command readiness.
  • “Search Connection Request” and “Backlink Suggestions” as pages — become actions inside SEO/Google.
  • “Website & Domain” as separate request page — move into Connections or Website Builder.
  • Duplicate Quotes & Invoices surfaces — single document model with clear status.
  • Empty “Records & Archive” shell — replace with archive filters on relevant lists.
  • Multiple “Save service request” forms (Phone Numbers, Calls & SMS, AI Receptionist) — single Communications Setup
  wizard.
  • Legacy founder routes once fully migrated.
  • Redundant approval checkboxes that duplicate automation rules — single source of truth.


4. Design, Typography, Compactness & Multi-Device Experience
Current impression (from inventory + live sign-in + zero-state capture): functional but dense. Recommend a cleaner visual
system.

  • Typography: Use a modern system stack or Inter / Source Sans 3. Base 16 px on desktop, 15–16 px on mobile. Clear
  hierarchy (H1 24–28 px, H2 18–20 px, body 15–16 px). Increase line-height to 1.5. Avoid tiny labels.
  • Spacing & density: Increase padding in cards and forms. Use progressive disclosure (accordions, tabs, “More” for
  advanced fields). Empty states should show the single next best action with a primary button.
  • Desktop: Left rail navigation (collapsible). Main content max-width ~1200 px. Command Centre as true dashboard with
  3–4 key cards + attention list. Sticky AI command bar.
  • Mobile / tablet: Bottom nav or compact drawer for primary groups. Large touch targets (min 44 px). Job execution and
  Team Chat optimised for field use (photo capture, status updates, one-tap accept). Dark mode already present — polish
  contrast.
  • Visual language: Consistent status colours (green=ok, amber=attention, red=block). Clear approval badges. Subtle
  Australian compliance cues without clutter.
  • Accessibility: Sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels on all icon buttons, focus states.
  • Performance: Lazy-load heavy sections (SEO, Media). Skeleton loaders instead of blank.


5. Security, Isolation & Backend Integrity (Hardening)
  • Enforce tenant_id on every query and mutation; automated cross-tenant tests in CI.
   • Separate permission layers: business roles, custom feature grants, channel/resource grants, founder/operator identities.
   Never rely on UI hiding.
   • All consequential external actions require explicit approval with full audit (who, what, when, payload hash).
   • Offshore staff: recorded safeguards + limited scopes before any customer data visibility.
   • Secrets (Stripe, Google, WhatsApp tokens) never client-side; use short-lived server sessions.
   • Encryption at rest for bank details, credentials, sensitive notes. TLS everywhere.
   • MFA available (already present) — encourage for owners and recommend for managers.
   • Regular backup/restore drills visible in Founder Reliability. Tamper-evident activity log.
   • Rate limiting and anomaly detection on approvals and exports.


6. Competitive Positioning vs Top 3 (2026)
Top similar platforms: ServiceTitan (enterprise depth, high cost), Jobber (simplicity for small crews), Housecall Pro (balanced
power). Australian peers (ServiceM8, Tradify, Fergus) are narrower job-to-invoice tools. Oprun’s opportunity is to own the full
stack (operations + native AI + growth + compliance) while matching Jobber/Housecall Pro usability.

After redesign Oprun should be positioned as: “The AI-native operating system for Australian service businesses — simpler
daily flow than ServiceTitan, deeper automation and SEO than Jobber or Housecall Pro, with built-in approvals and
work-rights checks.”

8. Conclusion & Immediate Next Actions
The inventory is comprehensive and the live sign-in experience is professional and security-conscious. The main risk is user
overwhelm from page proliferation. Implementing the consolidations above, the design refinements, and the security checklist
will make Oprun both more usable and more defensible than the current top three field-service platforms while preserving its
Australian and AI-native advantages.

Recommended immediate sequence:

  • Validate the proposed 6–7 group navigation with 3–5 existing or prospective subscribers.
