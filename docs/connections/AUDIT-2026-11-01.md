## Main findings and fixes

1. Platform transport readiness was being reported as a subscriber-owned email/SMS connection. The public connection model now distinguishes platform readiness, subscriber authorisation, successful/partial checks and selected resources.
2. Token expiry could be recalculated when a stored token was read. Issued expiry is now preserved, and expired tokens refresh or require reconnection.
3. Stateless callback state lacked durable browser/session/single-use verification. Opaque hashed state now binds the organisation, user, active session, provider, exact callback and a supplemental HttpOnly Lax cookie. The primary session cookie remains Strict. The legacy TikTok callback is covered by its own exact cookie path. Replays, wrong browsers/providers, revoked membership/permission and superseded attempts are rejected.
4. Google discovery only covered Search Console in this screen. It now discovers GA4 and GTM independently and reports partial permission/API failures. Saved Google resources reconcile the existing SEO links; deselected resources stop being used by that workspace.
5. Subscriber credentials previously required operator work. Owner/admin setup forms now verify a Resend sender or Telnyx number before encrypted storage; keys are never echoed. Normal messaging, marketing campaigns, business mail and follow-ups resolve the workspace transport. Platform administrative mail retains its separate transport. An explicitly disconnected owned transport cannot silently fall back to platform credentials.
6. Signed tenant webhooks verify both the provider signature and message/recipient ownership before routing. A workspace signature cannot update another workspace’s receipt. Out-of-order signed receipts are queued with the verified workspace identity, then applied only after an owned message record exists. Pending receipts are retried by the existing tracking worker and retained for seven days.
7. The connection screen now provides inline setup, selected resources, guided redacted diagnostics and sourced/timestamped figures. Missing counters remain unavailable. Failed refreshes retain prior figures with stale labels. Refresh is explicit and cached for five minutes; this is not continuous real-time reporting.
8. Disconnect removes only this workspace’s stored credentials, selections and owned routing. It does not revoke a potentially shared provider app grant and disrupt sibling connectors. The owner can revoke that grant in provider connected-app settings.

## Known limits

- No deployment or real external account consent/test has been performed for this candidate. External approvals, paid API access, unavailable metrics and MFA cannot be repaired by an assistant or by collecting account passwords.
- The connection assistant is a deterministic diagnostic guide. It does not claim autonomous credential recovery, provider approval, or password/MFA access.
- Snapchat Public Profile insights, Gmail/Outlook mailbox sync, a complete per-content analytics history for every network, arbitrary CMS control, and universal publishing/reply actions are not delivered here. They need separately scoped adapters and provider approval.
- Legacy workspaces that have never opted into an owned email/SMS transport retain explicitly separate platform delivery compatibility; their public card is not labelled as their own connected account. No production records are rewritten automatically.
- The existing Google client discovery is bounded; large agency accounts may require pagination/narrowing work. Empty resources do not imply entitlement to every account.
- Signed inbound mail handles text/metadata; attachment ingestion and full mailbox history are outside this change.
