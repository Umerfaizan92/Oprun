CORE FLOW (this is the product)
1. Call arrives on Oprun number or forwarded number.
2. AI answers naturally, identifies intent, pulls knowledge from Oprun (services, hours, past jobs, customer history).
3. Creates/updates Customer. Creates Enquiry + Lead. Merges into Unibox as a thread.
4. If booking intent: checks availability, proposes slot, confirms, creates Booking/Job draft, triggers approval if required.
5. After call: SMS summary + push. AI drafts reply for Unibox (approval gate). AI follows up (SMS/email) until booking confirmed or closed.
6. New customer → saved. Existing → updated with call notes.
7. Warm transfer: briefs team member with full context before connecting. Escalates to owner team (no external human receptionist).
