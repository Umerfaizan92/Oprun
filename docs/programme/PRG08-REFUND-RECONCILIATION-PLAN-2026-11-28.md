# PRG-08 refund reconciliation procedure for approval

This is a procedure design. Claude owns independent review of the frozen payment candidate 86f4589cb1b80a84b457eba49ba122b63f34ee45. No production financial records were accessed.

1. **Obtain a case-specific approval.** Record the authorised tenant, date range, currencies, provider account, reason, named operator and independent reviewer. Obtain separate permission for reading customer financial records and for any later correction. Scope provider permissions to read-only evidence and use a restricted workspace.
2. **Preserve the hold and gather minimal evidence.** Collect immutable internal payment, refund, job, approval and event references with their timestamps and recorded units. Compare with authoritative provider payment/charge/refund IDs, status, amounts, currency, idempotency reference and settlement evidence when required. Preserve redacted evidence pointers and hashes. Keep credentials and customer details out of shared notes.
3. **Reconcile each case in a dry run.** Match tenant and provider account first, then payment and refund identity. Convert amounts exactly in the relevant payment currency. Compare individual succeeded receipts, provider cumulative totals, ledger refund totals, job balances and dependent invoice/finance entries. Record ordered and duplicate events, outstanding/pending refunds and prior manual adjustments. Flag missing identities, ambiguous timeouts, conflicting currencies, impossible stored precision, and any provider total inconsistent with the ledger. A charge-level total alone cannot identify a submitted request.
4. **Propose and review only evidenced corrections.** Classify each case as already applied, confirmed unapplied, explicitly failed, still pending, or unresolved. Require exact before/after figures, source receipts, effect on dependent records and a stable case ID. An independent finance reviewer approves the dry-run report. Keep unresolved holds in place. No automatic retries, replay, clamping, cross-tenant matching or new refund.
5. **Require separate approval for any live execution.** If authorised later, verify the reviewed baseline still matches, use one auditable and idempotent correction per case, reconcile dependent records and validate the outcome with a second reviewer. Stop on drift, concurrent webhook activity, conflicting evidence or a failed invariant. Any compensating action requires its own approval and must preserve provider receipts and original evidence.
