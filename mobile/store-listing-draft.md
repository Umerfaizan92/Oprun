# Oprun store listing draft

Name: Oprun

Short description: Your customers, jobs, team and approvals in one workspace.

Description:

Run your world with Oprun, the AI-assisted office manager for service businesses.
Manage enquiries, organise jobs and keep your team connected from your phone.
Review drafts and proposed actions before they leave your business.

Use your existing Oprun business account to access the features available to
your role and plan. Workers see their assigned work; owners and authorised
team members manage the wider business. Connected email, SMS and social
features require the relevant provider setup and permissions.

An internet connection is required. AI suggestions require human review.

Publisher must verify all feature claims against the signed release, complete
privacy/data safety and purchase disclosures, and replace this draft with the
final store-specific wording and device screenshots before submission.
