Integrations

Connect the accounts your business already uses. Each business connects its own accounts, and anything that posts, sends or pays waits for your approval.

Social and reviews

  • Facebook and Instagram

    Posts, comments and follower totals sync. Posts and comment replies wait for your approval.

  • TikTok

    Videos you approve are uploaded to your account.

  • YouTube

    Channel totals and comments sync. Replies wait for your approval.

  • X

    Posts and replies you approve, from your own account.

  • Snapchat

    Connect your account alongside your other channels.

  • Google Business Profile

    Reviews, posts and performance for your business locations. Review replies wait for your approval.

Messages and email

  • WhatsApp Business

    Messages arrive in the shared inbox with delivery receipts.

  • Gmail

    Email threads arrive in the inbox; replies are drafted for you to send.

  • Email and SMS

    Send from your business name, with consent checked before marketing messages go out.

Website and search

  • Your website

    Web enquiries become customer records and replies wait for your approval.

  • Google Search and Analytics

    Search Console and Google Analytics 4 figures for your own site, read-only.

Payments and meetings

  • Stripe

    Card and wallet payments go straight to your own Stripe account.

  • PayPal

    Customers can pay invoices with PayPal into your account.

  • Zoom

    Create and list meetings in your own Zoom account.

Service names and logos belong to their owners and are shown only to identify the service you connect. Each connection needs that service's own account and permissions.
