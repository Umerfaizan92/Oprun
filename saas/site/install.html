Install Oprun

Keep your office one tap away on your phone, tablet or computer.

Browser installation is available now. Google Play and App Store downloads will appear here after their releases are approved.

Open workspace

Checking this device…

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Oprun in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open Oprun in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Oprun if offered, or use the browser menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Computer

Use Chrome or Edge’s install option, or Safari’s Add to Dock on supported macOS versions.

An internet connection is required for your workspace, messages, files and approvals. Installing does not download private business records for offline access.
