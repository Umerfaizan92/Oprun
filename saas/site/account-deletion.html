Account and data deletion

You can request deletion without uninstalling or reinstalling Oprun.

Workspace owners

  1. Sign in to your Oprun workspace.
  2. Open Plans & Billing and find Account & Data Safety.
  3. Select Request account deletion.
  4. Enter the code sent to your verified email and type DELETE MY ACCOUNT.
  5. Review the confirmation before scheduling deletion. A seven-day cancellation window applies.

A trial ending does not automatically delete your workspace. Deleting an entire workspace affects its team and business records.

Team members or accounts you cannot access

Use the contact form to request deletion of your own account or personal information. Identify your account and business so support can verify the request. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

Retention obligations, backup handling and any exceptions must be reviewed under the Privacy Notice. Support can explain what applies before a request is completed.
